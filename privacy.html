Privacy policy

Early Learnings · Last updated 13 November 2026

Draft for review. This policy describes how the app works today. Replace the items in [brackets] and have it reviewed by a qualified professional before offering the product to daycares or the public.

Summary

Early Learnings does not collect, send or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app remembers stays on the device it is used on.

What is stored on the device

InformationWhyWhere
Settings: language, age group, Calm Mode, volume, session length, hidden activitiesSo the app opens the way the educator left itThis device only
Educator PINTo keep settings away from childrenThis device only
Activity levels and starsTo keep activities at a suitable difficultyThis device only
An optional first nameUsed only for spoken praise and name tracing, if an adult chooses to enter oneThis device only
DrawingsOnly kept while the drawing is open, unless an adult saves the picture to the device's photosThis device only

None of this information is transmitted to us or to anyone else. It is not linked to an individual child, and the app does not record who is using it.

What is not collected

No names (other than the optional first name above), no photos, no voice recordings, no location, no contact details, no device identifiers and no usage statistics. The app does not use the camera or the microphone.

Internet use

The app's files are downloaded from the website that hosts it when it is first opened or updated. Like any website, the hosting provider ([hosting provider, currently GitHub Pages]) may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses; we do not receive or use these. After the first visit the app works without an internet connection. The app contains no links to outside websites, no in-app purchases and no advertising.

Speech

Spoken instructions use the voice built into the device. The text is spoken on the device and is not sent to us.

Deleting information

An educator can clear levels and stars at any time with Settings › Reset. Removing the app from the Home Screen, or clearing website data in the device's settings, deletes everything the app has stored.

Children's privacy

The app is intended to be set up and supervised by an adult. Because no personal information is collected from children, no parental consent is requested inside the app.

Changes

If the product later adds accounts or an administration portal for licence management, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and it will describe exactly what information they use.
